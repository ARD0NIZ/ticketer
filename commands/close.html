/close

Close the current ticket and create a transcript

Description

The /close command closes the current ticket channel and generates a complete transcript of all messages. This is useful for archiving resolved support cases and maintaining records.

/close [reason]

Parameters

  • reason (optional): Brief explanation of why the ticket is being closed

How it works

  • Creates a complete transcript of all messages in the ticket
  • Sends the transcript to the configured transcripts channel
  • Deletes the ticket channel after a short delay
  • Notifies relevant parties that the ticket was closed

Permissions Required

  • Must be used in a ticket channel
  • Must have support role or be the ticket creator
